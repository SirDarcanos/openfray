---
title: Your account & what's saved
description: What works without an account, what signing in adds, where your game is stored, and how to delete it all.
---

OpenFray works with no account at all. Signing in is about **keeping** things, not
unlocking the app.

## Without an account

Open [the console](/console/) and run a fight straight away. You get the whole tracker:
creatures from the built-in books, players, initiative, effects, spells, group saves,
dice, and the compendium.

Your fight is held **in that browser tab**. Reload the page by accident, or your browser
crashes, and it's still there. Close the tab and it's gone — deliberately, so nothing of
yours is sitting on a server you never asked to use. OpenFray warns you before you close
a tab with a fight in it.

Two things do stick around on your own device without an account: your
[rule-set choices](/docs/reference/settings/#rule-sets) and whether you like light or
dark. Those are preferences, not game data.

## Signing in

Sign in with **Discord** or **Google** — there's no password to make or remember, and
OpenFray never sees one. It's free, and the first time you sign in your account is
created for you.

From then on, this is saved to your account and follows you between devices:

| What                              | Why you'd want it                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The fight you're running**      | close the laptop mid-session, pick it up next week, mid-round                |
| **Creatures and spells you make** | your homebrew library, on every device                                       |
| **Characters**                    | build the party once instead of retyping them each session                   |
| **Campaigns**                     | your table's [house rules](/docs/library/campaigns/), applied to every fight |

Saving happens quietly in the background while you play. You never wait for it, and
there's no save button.

Nothing you did before signing in is thrown away — the fight on your board stays put.

## Deleting everything

In **Profile**, "delete account" removes your account and everything attached to it: your
fights, creatures, spells, characters, and campaigns. You'll be asked to type your email
to confirm, because it can't be undone.

It's a real deletion, not a flag — the rows are gone, and so is the sign-in itself. If
you come back later with the same Discord or Google account, you start fresh.
