OpenFray keeps track of a fight for you — who acts when, everyone's hit points, the
conditions and effects on each creature, and the dice. It also has a built-in list of
creatures and spells to pull from. It runs in your web browser, and you don't need an
account. Signing in just lets you save your game.

These pages explain how each part works.
