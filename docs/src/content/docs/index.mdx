OpenFray is a combat console and initiative tracker for Dungeons and Dragons. It keeps track of a fight for
you: who acts when, everyone's hit points, the conditions and effects on each creature,
and the dice. It also has a built-in list of creatures and spells to pull from. It runs in
your web browser, and you don't need an account. Signing in just lets you save your game.

These pages explain how each part works. New here? Start with
[Getting started](/docs/getting-started/), then come back for the rest.
